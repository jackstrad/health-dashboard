Health Coach questions are processed in memory, cleared after answering, and are not saved or uploaded. Privacy model

Nothing is uploaded.

Check-ins and imported labs are stored in this browser’s local storage. Health Coach questions are answered in memory, cleared after answering, and are not saved or uploaded. Browser and cache storage are shared across this site’s origin, not isolated by URL path. The hosted files contain no personal health baseline.

Exported JSON backups are private but unencrypted. Protect them and export before clearing browser data or changing devices.
